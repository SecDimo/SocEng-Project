We are reaching out to gather employee feedback as part of the college's ongoing efforts to improve the campus experience for everyone. Your perspective as a Macomb Community College employee is important to us.

We are hosting a series of employee focus groups in May to better understand your experiences at Macomb Community College. To help us plan these sessions, we are asking all employees to complete a brief survey that should take less than three minutes.

Your responses will be completely confidential and will not be shared with supervisors or department heads. All participants who complete the survey will be entered into a drawing for one of five $50 gift cards.

The survey will close on Friday at 5:00 PM.
